Vending machine placement FAQ

Answers for business owners

Do vending machine companies pay you? STASH does. Hosting is free, and your business earns 10% of net sales every month. Here are the questions owners ask before they say yes.

Cost and pay

Do vending machine companies pay you?

STASH does. You earn 10% of net sales every month, with terms set in a written host agreement.

How much does it cost to host a vending machine?

Nothing. STASH provides the machine at no cost to you, and we install, stock and service it.

What does net sales mean?

What customers pay at the machine, minus sales tax, payment processing fees and any money returned to customers. See how the revenue share works.

Why is the host share 10%?

Because hosting costs you nothing and takes none of your time. You don't stock the machine, track inventory, manage it or handle customers. STASH pays for the machine, the products, card processing fees, taxes, restocking, repairs and customer support. See how the revenue share works.

How much does a vending machine make?

It depends on foot traffic, your hours and the product mix, so we don't promise a number. Your share is always 10% of net sales. See what drives it.

Is it worth having a vending machine in my business?

If you have steady foot traffic and a spare wall or corner, it gives your customers a 24/7 amenity and gives you a monthly revenue share, with no cost, no inventory and no staff time on your side.

The machine

How much space does it need?

Slim Wall: 23.6 inches wide, 40.5 inches high and 10.2 inches deep. Stand Model: 23.6 inches wide, 64 inches high and 10.2 inches deep.

What does my business need to provide?

A spot with steady foot traffic, a standard power outlet, Wi-Fi and access for our team to restock and service the machine.

Who pays for the electricity?

The host. The machine plugs into a standard outlet at your location.

What if something breaks?

We handle all maintenance, service and payment issues.

Who do customers contact with a problem?

STASH. Every machine has a code customers scan to reach our support, and we reply within 2 business days.

Products

What products can it carry?

Trading cards, phone chargers, power banks, earbuds, travel essentials and personal-care items, depending on the location and the machine format.

Who stocks the machine?

We do. STASH manages inventory, restocking and product rotation.

Can we request products?

Yes. We adjust the product mix based on your location and customer needs.

Getting started

How does vending machine placement work?

We find the right spot in your business, choose the products for your crowd, install and run the machine, and pay you every month. See the four steps.

What kinds of businesses can host a machine?

Apartment complexes, hotel lobbies, gyms, offices, hospitals, bars, nightclubs, liquor stores, arcades, schools and campuses, gas stations and other high-traffic properties.

Is there a long contract?

No. There is no minimum term. Either side can end the agreement with 30 days' notice, and we come and collect the machine.

Do you place vending machines near me?

We're placing new machines every month. Tell us where your business is and we'll let you know how soon we can get one to you.

Free to host. We pay you 10% of net sales.
