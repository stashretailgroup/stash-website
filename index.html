Straight answers

Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost?

STASH provides the machine at no cost to you.

How do we make money?

You earn 10% of net sales every month.

Who stocks it?

We do. STASH manages inventory, restocking and product rotation.

What if something breaks?

We handle all maintenance, service and payment issues.

Can we request products?

Yes. We adjust the product mix based on your location and customer needs.

What products can it carry?

Trading cards, vapes and other high-demand products, based on local regulations. Vape machines go only in 21+ locations.

How much space does it need?

What do you need from us?

A suitable location, access for servicing, an electrical outlet and Wi-Fi.

Where can it be placed?

Apartments, gyms, schools, hotels, offices, bars, nightclubs, gas stations, hospitals, campuses and other high-traffic properties.

Which areas do you serve?

We're placing new machines every month. Tell us where your business is and we'll let you know how soon we can get one to you.
