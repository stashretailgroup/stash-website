Straight answers

Questions from apartments

Where does a vending machine go in an apartment complex?

Lobbies, mail rooms, clubhouses, fitness rooms and laundry rooms all work. It needs a wall or a corner, a standard outlet and Wi-Fi.

Who do residents contact if something goes wrong?

STASH. Every machine has a code residents scan to report a problem, and we reply within 2 business days.

What does it cost the property?

Nothing. The machine, installation, stock and service are all provided by STASH.

How is the property paid?

The property earns 10% of net sales, paid monthly. Terms are set in a written host agreement with no minimum term. See how the revenue share works.
