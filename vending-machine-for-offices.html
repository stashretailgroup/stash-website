Straight answers

Questions from offices

What goes in a vending machine for an office?

Electronics and everyday essentials such as chargers, earbuds and personal-care items. For a break room, a Smart Fridge or Smart Market can add grab-and-go items.

Who signs up, the tenant or the building?

Whoever controls the space. We work with office owners, building owners and property managers who have the authority to place the machine.

What does the office need to provide?

A spot with steady foot traffic, a standard power outlet, Wi-Fi and access for our team to restock and service the machine.

How is the office paid?

The office or building earns 10% of net sales, paid monthly. Terms are set in a written host agreement with no minimum term. See how the revenue share works.
