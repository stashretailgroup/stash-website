Straight answers

Questions from hotels

What goes in a vending machine for a hotel lobby?

Phone chargers and cables, power banks, travel essentials such as toothbrushes and razors, and personal-care basics. We adjust the mix to what your guests buy.

Can it take the place of a front-desk sundry shop?

It can take that job off the front desk. Guests serve themselves with a tap or card, and STASH handles stock, service and support.

Do you work with independent and franchise hotels?

Yes. We work directly with the owner or the person who makes decisions for the property.

How is the hotel paid?

The hotel earns 10% of net sales, paid monthly. Terms are set in a written host agreement with no minimum term. See how the revenue share works.
