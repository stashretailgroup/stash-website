Straight answers

Questions from hosts

What is a trading card vending machine?

A self-serve machine with a touchscreen that sells sealed trading card products, such as Pokémon booster packs and sports card packs, by tap or card.

Which sets does a Pokémon card vending machine carry?

Current sets such as Ascended Heroes, Prismatic Evolutions and 151, depending on what is available. We rotate stock as new sets release.

Is a booster pack vending machine right for my business?

It suits places where people wait, play or hang out: arcades, game rooms, entertainment venues, bars and liquor stores.

Do I buy the cards?

No. STASH buys and owns all the stock. You host the machine and earn 10% of net sales.
