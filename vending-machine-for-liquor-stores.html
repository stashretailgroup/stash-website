Straight answers

Questions from liquor stores

What does a vending machine for a liquor store sell?

Trading card packs, sports cards, phone chargers and power banks. We choose the mix with you and adjust it to what sells.

Will it compete with what I already sell?

It shouldn't. We stock products most liquor stores don't carry, and we change the mix if you ask.

Does it tie up my register?

No. The machine takes payment itself, and customers with a problem contact STASH through the code on the machine.

How is the store paid?

Your store earns 10% of net sales, paid monthly. Terms are set in a written host agreement with no minimum term. See how the revenue share works.
