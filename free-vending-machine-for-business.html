Straight answers

Questions about hosting

Is the vending machine really free?

Yes. STASH owns the machine and provides it at no cost. There is no purchase, lease, rental fee or stocking fee.

How do I get paid to host a vending machine?

You earn 10% of the machine's net sales, paid every month. You choose how you're paid when you sign the host agreement. See how the revenue share works.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. There is no minimum term. Either side can end the agreement with 30 days' notice, and we come and collect the machine.

What does my business need to provide?

A spot with steady foot traffic, a standard power outlet, Wi-Fi and access for our team to restock and service the machine.

Do you offer vending machine placement near me?

We're placing new machines every month. Tell us where your business is and we'll let you know how soon we can get one to you.
